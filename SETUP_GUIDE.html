Products

Set up Gumroad — Sell Your Products

Gumroad is free to join and lets you sell physical or digital products instantly. They handle payments, delivery emails, and taxes. You keep ~91% of every sale.

🛍
Gumroad FREE
Sign up → Create a product for each item → Copy the link → Paste in Nova.js config
SIGN UP →

After creating each product on Gumroad, open Nova.js and paste the links in the config at the very top:

const NOVA_CONFIG = {
  products: {
    1: 'https://gumroad.com/l/abc123',  // Midnight Satin Slip Dress
    2: 'https://gumroad.com/l/def456',  // High Rise Sculpt Cargo
    // ... one per product
  },

Once configured, clicking "Add to Bag" then "Checkout" with a single item will open the real Gumroad payment page in a new tab.

02
VIP Subscription & Gift Cards

Set up Stripe — Recurring Revenue

Stripe handles recurring subscriptions (your VIP membership at $2.99/month) and one-time payments (gift cards). Create a free account, then create Payment Links — no coding required.

💳
Stripe FREE TO START
dashboard.stripe.com → Payment Links → Create → Copy link → Paste in config
SIGN UP →

For VIP membership: Create a recurring price of $2.99/month or $35.88/year. Then paste the link:

  vipMonthly: 'https://buy.stripe.com/your_link_here',
  vipAnnual: 'https://buy.stripe.com/your_link_here',

For Gift Cards: Create 5 one-time payment links ($25, $50, $100, $250, $500):

  giftCard: {
    25: 'https://buy.stripe.com/gift_25_link',
    50: 'https://buy.stripe.com/gift_50_link',
    // ...
  },
03
Email List

Set up Mailchimp — Build Your Audience

Every email you collect (newsletter, exit popup, spin wheel) is a potential future sale. Mailchimp is free up to 500 contacts and lets you send promotional emails to your list.

📧
Mailchimp FREE up to 500
mailchimp.com → Audience → Signup Forms → Embedded → copy action URL
SIGN UP →

After getting your form action URL, paste it in the config:

  mailchimpUrl: 'https://yourname.us1.list-manage.com/subscribe/post?u=XXX&id=XXX',

Now every email entered in the newsletter, exit popup, and spin wheel goes straight to your Mailchimp list automatically.
